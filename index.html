ABOUT THE INSTITUTE

A focused place to
prepare for what’s next.

Amar Jyoti Coaching Centre Pvt. Ltd. provides a platform for students to excel in the Government sector. The institute offers preparation for Bank, Railway, SSC, Defence, Psycho-Test and Interview examinations.

Previously known mainly for Mathematics tuition, the institute expanded into coaching for Bank, SSC, RRB, NDA, Air Force, Navy and other competitive examinations from 2016. Its approach brings together quality academics, experienced faculty, a positive learning environment, performance analysis and personal attention.

Concept clarity and systematic preparation
